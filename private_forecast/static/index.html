What this view can tell you

These are forecasts collected at the same moment, with each provider’s original issue time shown. Temperature and wind use WeatherNext’s average; precipitation uses its median—the middle outcome.

The cloud currently saves selected hours for accuracy checks. Lines stop where hours are missing; blank space does not mean calm or dry weather. Daily high/low and precipitation totals need a complete forecast timeline and are not calculated here.

Differences show what the models expect, not which will be correct. Shaded ranges describe forecast possibilities; they are not a guarantee. For measured results, see the public accuracy scorecard.

The typical gap is the median absolute difference at shared saved hours. “Show largest gap” selects the biggest difference in this window; it does not identify a forecast error.

Re-read uses the cloud state already fetched to this PC. Refresh instructions are in PRIVATE_FORECAST.md. This viewer makes no forecast requests, changes no history, and keeps future values out of the public site.
